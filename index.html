⸻   Our Story   ⸻

A Family-Run Sanctuary

Thekkan's Haven is a labor of love managed by Rony T Babu and Family. Located in the peaceful village of Neyyassery near Thodupuzha, our 8-acre estate offers a genuine slice of Kerala's natural beauty.

We built this haven to provide a private, safe, and relaxing space for families and groups. When you book with us, the entire property is yours—ensuring total privacy and a truly home-like experience.
